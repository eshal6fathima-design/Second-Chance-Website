Our Services

Safe Shelter

Confidential emergency housing for women and children fleeing abuse. Secure location, 24/7 support, and basic necessities provided.

Learn more →

Therapy & Counseling

Professional trauma-informed therapy, support groups, and mental health services to help survivors heal and regain strength.

Learn more →

Education & Jobs

Skills training, GED preparation, computer classes, and job placement assistance to help women achieve financial independence.

Learn more →

Child Sponsorship

Monthly sponsorship provides education, nutritious meals, healthcare, and safe after-school programs for children in need.

Learn more →

Legal Advocacy

Free legal counseling and representation for survivors navigating protection orders, custody, and other legal matters.

Learn more →

24/7 Helpline

Confidential crisis support, information, and referrals available anytime. Call or text for immediate assistance.
